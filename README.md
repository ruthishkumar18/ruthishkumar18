# 🚀 Featured Engineering

## 🌿 PlantSpeakAI

**AI-enabled plant electrophysiology monitoring system**

A plant-health monitoring system based on bioelectrical signals collected from plants.

**Stack:** `ESP32` `Ag/AgCl Electrodes` `Python` `Machine Learning` `Neural Networks` `Android`

**Goal:** Detect plant stress conditions such as heat, cold, water-related stress, mechanical stress and pest-related stress from plant electrical responses.

---

## 🔩 Steel Surface Defect Detection

**Deep-learning based industrial inspection**

A computer-vision pipeline for detecting surface defects in steel.

**Stack:** `YOLO11` `OpenCV` `Python` `Roboflow` `Deep Learning`

**Goal:** Automate visual inspection and identify defects consistently.

**Goal:** Improve inspection and counting workflows through computer vision.
